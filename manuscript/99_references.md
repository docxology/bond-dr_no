# References

[Kennedy and others 2011] W. E. Kennedy, T. J. Strenge, and K. A. Pauley,
*DCAL and the RADTRAN Family of Codes*, assessment of radionuclide dose and
inventory (reference text for inhalation dose coefficients).

[Turner 1994] D. B. Turner, *Workbook of Atmospheric Dispersion Estimates*,
Lewis Publishers, 2nd ed. (Pasquill–Gifford dispersion methodology).

[Martin & Green 1976] J. A. Martin and D. A. Green, "Dispersion and Dosimetry
Models", EPA guidance on Gaussian plume parameterisation.

[Bateman 1910] H. Bateman, "The Solution of a System of Differential Equations
Occurring in the Theory of Radioactive Transformations", *Proceedings of the
Cambridge Philosophical Society*, 15:423–427 (Bateman equation for decay chains).

[Currie 1968] L. A. Currie, "Limits for Qualitative Detection and Quantitative
Determination", *Analytical Chemistry* 40(3):586–593 (the L_D/MDA formalism).

[NIST XCOM] M. J. Berger, J. H. Hubbell, S. M. Seltzer et al., *XCOM: Photon
Cross Sections Database*, NIST SRD 8 (mass attenuation coefficients).

[Knoll 2010] G. F. Knoll, *Radiation Detection and Measurement*, 4th ed.,
John Wiley & Sons (detector resolution, specific gamma-ray constants).

[ICRP 119] International Commission on Radiological Protection, *Compendium of
Dose Coefficients based on ICRP Publication 60*, ICRP Publication 119 (inhalation
dose coefficients).

[BOND suite] PROJECT BOND suite documentation and the frozen `bond-api`
mission protocol, internal to this working tree.
